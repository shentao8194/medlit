# MedLit

Adapted from https://github.com/cookjohn/pm-skills and https://github.com/jyluo1994/paper-acquisition-anti-scrape.

Use official PubMed or NCBI E-utilities. Translate PICO into MeSH and title/abstract terms, OR synonyms and AND concepts. Apply study and date filters as warranted. Record executed query, date, URL and verified hit count. Paginate as needed. Export cited records with PMID, DOI, title, journal, year and full-text URL as CSV or NBIB when requested. Deduplicate by PMID or DOI and link secondary reports to their parent trial. Label web-index results separately from official PubMed results.

Resolve records by PMID, DOI or verified title. Check open PDFs at PMC and the publisher. For subscription articles, use the connected local browser on the authorized campus network, VPN or library proxy. Follow the browser tool instructions. Let the user complete institution login or human verification in that browser. Use publisher PDF and supplement controls. Verify download and title or DOI, recording source, filename and status. Distinguish files on the local device from those accessible in the workspace. If access is unavailable, give the verified link and report the reason.
